Cyberattacks are evolving fast, and so is the need to modernize SecOps. Join the SecOps Virtual Summit to learn how to detect threats at the point of attack and harness agentic AI to accelerate SOC investigation and response.

During this Summit, you'll learn:

Stay Ahead of AI-Driven Risk

See how AI is reshaping the threat landscape and what it means for the future of security operations.

Modernize Your SecOps

Modernization is a journey. Get the insights you need to recognize and act on your priority needs.

See AI-Native SecOps in Action

Explore use cases and customer outcomes that show how AI helps security teams investigate and respond faster.

Who Should Attend this Summit?

Modernize Security Operations: Wherever You Are in the Journey

This event is built for CISOs, SOC managers, incident responders, insider risk managers, and security teams. Anyone part of developing foundational detection or looking to connect, automate, and scale mature SOCs with AI should attend.

Bonus: Attend live and earn Continuing Professional Education (CPE) credits while gaining practical insights from IDC, Fortinet experts, and real-world customer scenarios. Please see the FAQ document for complete eligibility details.

  • Derek Manky

  • Duncan Brown
    Group Vice President, Global Domain Lead: Worldwide Security & Trust
    IDC

  • Robert May
    Executive Vice President of Technology and Product Management
    Fortinet

    Vice President of Global IT
